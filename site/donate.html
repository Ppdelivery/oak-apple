Why this exists

Oak apple is free to use, and it collects nothing about you — no servers, no account, no analytics. It carries one advertisement, on the settings screens behind your code, and a one-time purchase that removes it; neither home screen ever shows one. That is the whole of what earns behind it. If the app is useful to you and you want to chip in beyond that, this is how.

What a donation does

Nothing inside the app. Giving unlocks no features, lifts no limits, and is not recorded anywhere the app can see. It is a thank-you, not a purchase, and it is not the one-time unlock — that is bought through Google Play, inside the app, and is the only thing that changes how Oak apple behaves. Give or not, the app is identical, and it has no way to find out either way.

How

Donations are handled by Buy Me a Coffee, which takes cards as well as Google Pay and Apple Pay. Nothing is recurring unless you choose it there.

About that link

It leaves this site, and that is the whole design. This page loads nothing from anywhere — no fonts, no scripts, no analytics, no cookies, no donation widget — exactly like the privacy policy. A payment button embedded here would pull a third party's code onto the same address as that policy, and the policy would stop being true.

Buy Me a Coffee is a separate company with its own privacy policy and its own cookies. What happens after you follow the link is between you and them. The app is told nothing about it: it never contacts Buy Me a Coffee, and has no way to learn who gave or whether anyone did. I see only what a supporter chooses to put on Buy Me a Coffee, and nothing else reaches me.
