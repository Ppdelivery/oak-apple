Oak apple

A home screen for Android. app.homedeck.launcher

What it is

Oak apple replaces your phone's home screen. It can move apps you choose into an Android work profile, and show a plain home screen that does not list them. What you keep to yourself stays behind a code you set.

What it does not do

There are no servers, no account and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is reported anywhere, and what it keeps, it keeps on your phone. It works with no connection at all — nothing waits on the network, and no feature switches off when there is no signal.

The two exceptions are stated plainly, and only ever happen from behind your code: an advertisement on the settings screens, which Google serves, and the one-time purchase that removes them, which Google Play handles. The privacy policy sets out exactly what each one tells Google, and what it does not.

Arranging your home screen

Oak apple can write down what your phone has and where everything sits, hand that to an AI assistant along with a sentence about how you want it arranged, and read the answer back. The app never talks to the assistant and the assistant never touches your phone — two files cross a gap neither side can see across. How that works, and the prompt to copy.

Privacy

The privacy policy is the full account: what is collected, what is stored, where it is stored, and why each permission is asked for.

Support

Oak apple is free, and nothing here is asked for. If it is useful to you and you want to chip in beyond the one-time purchase, there is a donation page. It unlocks nothing in the app, and the app is never told whether anyone gave — which is rather the point of it.
